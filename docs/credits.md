- **License key:** _none yet_. Without one, every feature works, but the grid shows a "For evaluation only" watermark and the browser console prints a license notice. To license, put the key in `.env` as `VITE_AG_GRID_LICENSE_KEY` and record here the license type, the date, and where the key came from (AG Grid offers educational keys; check eligibility).
